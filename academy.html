About the academy

More than learning software. Learn production.

The rocksKING Music & Media Academy offers a flexible approach to music production training. Students can choose the topics they want to learn and follow the academic calendar to plan their sessions.

The programme assumes students already have fundamental music and computing skills. Where extra instruction is needed, it may use part of the scheduled session.

Before you enroll

Important Notes

01

Prerequisites

This is not a general music or computer training class. Students are expected to have fundamental music and computing skills. Basic concepts are not the main focus of the programme.

02

Additional instruction

If a student needs foundational music or computer instruction, it may be provided during a scheduled session. This reduces time available for the main course, and additional sessions may need to be purchased.

03

Flexible learning

Students may choose the topics they wish to learn. Check the academic calendar to find out when those topics will be taught and plan sessions accordingly.
